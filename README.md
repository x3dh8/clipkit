# ClipKit

Turn gaming clips into vertical videos for TikTok, Shorts and Reels.

- **Clip:** vertical crop, facecam + gameplay split, or fit with a blurred background. Trim, auto-captions with word highlighting, export.
- **Highlights:** scans a long clip for bursts of action, flashes and loud game sounds.
- **Montage:** cuts several clips to the beat of a song, with zoom punches and flashes.

Everything runs in the browser. Videos never leave your device. Captions use Whisper through transformers.js, downloaded on first use.

- `index.html`: phone version (installable: Add to Home Screen)
- `desktop.html`: computer version (open in Chrome)
